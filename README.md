📊 Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

🎯 Objective :

To analyze sales performance across products, regions, and time periods, enabling stakeholders to track revenue trends, identify top-performing segments, and improve data-driven decision-making.

🧩 Project Overview :

This project involves building an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of total sales, profit, quantity sold, and customer trends. It helps business users quickly understand performance patterns and identify growth opportunities.

⚙️ Tools & Technologies :

Power BI

Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)

DAX (Calculated Measures & KPIs)

Microsoft Excel (Data Source)

📌 Key KPIs :

Total Sales

Total Profit

Total Quantity Sold

Profit Margin

Sales Growth Trend

📈 Dashboard Features :

Interactive slicers (Date, Region, Product Category)

Dynamic KPIs for real-time insights

Sales and profit trend analysis

Top-performing products and regions

Clean and user-friendly dashboard layout

💡 Business Insights :

Certain product categories contribute the highest share of revenue and profit

Sales peak during specific months, indicating seasonal demand patterns

A small group of products generates the majority of total sales

Regional performance varies significantly, highlighting growth opportunities
